# Organizing Personal Health Care with AI

**[Open the live interactive guide](https://az9713.github.io/organizing-personal-health-care/)**

A practical, source-linked tutorial for using AI to organize health records, prepare appointments, research questions responsibly, coordinate support, and protect quality of life. It adapts a cancer-care workflow for prevention and ongoing conditions.

## Inspiration and thanks

Inspired by Peter Yang’s video, **[How I’m Using ChatGPT to Help My Mom Fight Cancer — 5 Steps](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ddc5NFNnCfE)**, and his open-source **[F-Cancer workflow](https://github.com/petergyang/fuck-cancer)**.

Thank you, Peter, for sharing your experience, practical approach, and free resources with patients and caregivers. The tutorial follows the full video transcript and distinguishes Peter’s documented examples from added templates, explanations, and broader health-maintenance adaptations. It is an independent educational resource, with no affiliation implied.

## What is included

- Five-step interactive workflow with links to the corresponding video chapters.
- Separate pathways for cancer care, ongoing conditions, and prevention.
- A records explorer explaining written reports, original imaging, and pathology material.
- A follow-up example showing why a request needs confirmation and an agreed action.
- An absolute-versus-relative-risk exercise using clearly labeled invented numbers.
- A brief formatter, fictional example, blank template, copy/download controls, and printing.
- The complete reference guide with copyable prompts and official sources.
- A dark, responsive layout, reduced-motion support, and an offline HTML download.

## Use it

1. Open the [live guide](https://az9713.github.io/organizing-personal-health-care/).
2. Choose the pathway matching your purpose.
3. Draft a brief from verified records and prepare three questions or actions.
4. Review clinical facts and instructions with the appropriate healthcare professional.

You can also download `index.html` and open it in a modern browser. The HTML includes its styles, scripts, and guide content, so no build step or server is required. External source links still require an internet connection. Clipboard access depends on the browser; text can be copied manually when necessary.

## Privacy and medical boundaries

The published files contain no real reader or owner patient records, personal contact details, or private ChatGPT Page/Site links. All built-in patient examples are fictional. Public source attributions and resource names are retained.

The brief formatter does not call an AI service or upload entries. It keeps typed entries in the current page’s memory; reloading clears them. An explicit download creates a file on the visitor’s device. There are no analytics, cookies, or application storage for these entries. Avoid entering identifying information on a shared device and store downloaded health information appropriately.

This tool supports education, preparation, and coordination. It does not diagnose, select treatment, determine trial eligibility, or replace a care team. For urgent or life-threatening symptoms, use emergency services and follow the treating team’s instructions.

## Validation

The workflow selections, records explorer, follow-up stages, risk calculations, fictional brief population, and reference navigation were checked in a browser. The risk exercise was checked at a 1-in-100 baseline: a 20% relative reduction gives a 0.2-percentage-point absolute reduction and a number needed to treat of 500. These are illustrative mathematics, not clinical estimates.
